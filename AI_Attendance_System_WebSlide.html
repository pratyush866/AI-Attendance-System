Problem Statement
Proxy Attendance & Classroom Bunking Crisis
Slide 02 / 09
⚠️ Conventional Attendance Flaws
  • Manual Registers: 10–15 mins wasted per class (20-25% teaching loss).
  • Paper Tampering: Registers get damaged, misplaced, or falsely marked.
  • WhatsApp QR Leaks: QR codes are forwarded to absent students outside.
  • GPS Mock Spoofing: Android Fake GPS apps mark attendance from canteens.
  • Peer Proxy: Friends sign for absent bunking classmates.
  • Clerical Burden: Hours spent compiling monthly DDU reports.
🎯 Project Goal & Engineering Focus
  • Core Challenge: Prove physical in-class presence with zero hardware cost.
  • Zero Proxy Guarantee: Block QR forwarding, location spoofing, photo replay.
  • Anti-Bunk Cross-Check: Presence verified against faculty room photo.
  • Dual-Biometrics: Deep metric face recognition + voice fallback.
  • Zero Dedicated Hardware: Works on existing phones, laptops & web browsers.
  • Dual Database: Supabase PostgreSQL cloud sync + local SQLite replica.
Proposed Architecture
Dual-Verification Anti-Bunk Protocol
Slide 03 / 09
1️⃣ Step 1: Student Live Selfie
  • Faculty launches session with dynamic 4-digit PIN & QR.
  • Student enters PIN + snaps live webcam selfie.
  • Dlib ResNet-34 extracts 128D facial vector.
  • Verifies identity against profile (\(d \le 0.58\)).
  • Status: Claim Recorded (Pending Room Check).
2️⃣ Step 2: Faculty Room Photo
  • Faculty snaps a single wide-angle classroom photo.
  • Captures all seated students at exact timestamp.
  • Uploaded directly into AI verification queue.
  • Zero special hardware—uses phone or laptop camera.
3️⃣ Step 3: AI Cross-Matching
  • ResNet-34 extracts all faces in room picture.
  • Computes Euclidean distance matrix with selfie.
  • Matched in Room: PRESENT (Verified).
  • Missing from Room: PROXY / BUNK DETECTED!

Biometric AI Pipeline
Deep Metric Learning & Audio Forensics Pipeline
Slide 07 / 09
👁️ Face Recognition (ResNet-34)
  • Face Detection: HOG & CNN MMOD detectors for varied classroom lighting.
  • 68-Point Alignment: Normalizes eye, nose, and mouth positions via affine transform.
  • 128D Metric Embedding: Maps features onto a unit hypersphere.
  • Euclidean Metric: \(d(v_1, v_2) = \sqrt{\sum (v_{1,i} - v_{2,i})^2}\).
  • Threshold: \(d \le 0.58\) ensures 99.38% LFW accuracy.
🎙️ Voice Biometrics (GE2E)
  • 16 kHz Audio Input: Captured via browser microphone.
  • Spectrogram: Librosa STFT computes 40-band mel spectrograms.
  • GE2E 3-Layer LSTM: Generates 256D speaker d-vector.
  • Cosine Metric: \(S_c = \frac{u \cdot v}{\|u\| \|v\|} \ge 0.75\).
  • Accessibility: Hands-free attendance check-in.
🛡️ Liveness & Security Guard
  • Anti-Replay Defense: Live browser webcam capture blocks static photos.
  • Dynamic 4-Digit PIN: Changes every lecture; expires automatically.
  • Dynamic QR Token: Segno QR regenerated on teacher screen.
  • Duplicate Guard: Database constraint blocks repeat check-in.
  • Biometric Privacy: Stores 128D float vectors; raw photos purged.
